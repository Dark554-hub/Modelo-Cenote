# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Field engineers, environmental researchers, and local community members monitoring the health of water in cenotes (natural sinkholes in Yucatán, Mexico). They need a reliable, high-contrast, professional tool that operates in both connected and disconnected (offline field) environments.

## Product Purpose
Real-time water quality monitoring, automated Machine Learning diagnostics, and compliance checking for NOM-127-SSA1-2021 and NOM-001-SEMARNAT standards. It aims to prevent contamination, protect ecosystems, and facilitate clean water management.

## Brand Personality
Scientific, expert, precise, professional, clean. A premium "tech-organic" tool. Serious but modern; avoiding cheap marketing buzzwords or generic SaaS look-and-feel.

## Anti-references
- Saturated generic purple-to-blue gradients.
- Playful emoji decorations.
- Rounded boxes wrapping every element (over-encapsulation).
- Side-stripe thick borders.
- Low-contrast typography.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over Clutter**: Focus on data legibility and typography hierarchy, not boxing everything in cards.
- **Scientific Authority**: Display information with precise notation, professional SVG icons, and clear visual states.
- **Robustness**: Build a UI that feels reliable, indicating live data feeds and local synchronization states cleanly.
- **Atmospheric Off-white**: A crisp, professional, high-contrast palette without relying on warm parchment/cream background clichés.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1 for body copy.
- Clear indicators for online/offline states.
- Responsive grids that adapt to mobile field screens and desktop analysis centers.
